# The vault's rule records

Every house rule the vault holds is a rule record in [rules/](rules/), one file per coverage ledger area, and lives there and nowhere else (#154, #265): other files cite a record by its id and never repeat its wording. A gate question is a record the building session or the gate critics answer, and one No fails a candidate. A project's own gate questions live in its own gate file, in this same form, and `node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/gate.js check <gate file or directory>…` refuses a file that breaks it. Give it the gate files whose ids the checked file names; a directory stands for every `.md` file in it. `gate.js list --asset <class> [--fact <fact>]… ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-critique/rules [<project gate>]` is the selector (#273): it hands each reader only the records whose asset condition holds, leaving out every staged record unless `--staged` is given. Its default reader, `--reader gate`, joins the script, code and look questions into one numbered list and reports each clash: two questions on the list that write the same property where neither yields to the other, or each does. A record answered by a script that is `owed` joins the look questions, marked "script owed". With `--look` it prints the look questions alone, the list a critic is given, cut where there are more than 20 into as many passes of at most 20 as it needs, function and role before treatment and value, then by area (#225, #414). `--reader builder --stage direction` gives the targets of every function and role record, `--stage build` adds the treatment records for the parts the candidate has (`--parts`, below) and the records the builder answers at `value` or with no stage, and `--stage finish` the scripts to run. `--reader whole-page` and `type` give the records each critic answers and the reference sections they cite, and `--reader script` every record a script answers, with its script.

**The form.** One `### <id>` heading per record: the heading is its `id`, unique across every gate file and prefixed by its owner (`vault.`, or the project's library key and a dot). Under it, one `- <field>: <value>` line per field, and nothing else. Records are read by agents and scripts, so they stay in the technical register. Every question gives these eight fields:

- `applies-when`: the asset condition, then `; ` and the part condition the critic reads. The asset condition is `any`, or terms joined all by ` and ` or all by ` or `, each one of: `web-surface`, `brand-art`, `motion`, `identity`, `print`, `social` (the asset's class); `has-motion`, `has-motion-over-5s`, `has-brand-motion`, `has-cta`, `has-payment-control`, `has-traced-mark` (facts the round declares about the asset; `has-brand-motion` where any of its motion is brand motion, as design-round §4 defines it). A `motion` asset, `has-motion-over-5s` and `has-brand-motion` each need `has-motion` too, and a `motion` asset needs `has-brand-motion`.
- `question`: answerable Yes or No; Yes passes.
- `answered-by`: `script` (a measuring script's output is the answer), `code` (the code, read once), `look` (the gate critics, from the render), `whole-page` (the whole-page critic), `type` (the type critic, a fresh subagent given only the type records and the brief's type, #258) or `builder` (a target the building session builds to, with no check).
- `writes`: the one property the question decides.
- `yields-to`: what this question gives way to on a clash, comma-separated: question ids, or `level <n>` for that precedence level and every level above it, or `none`. The levels are the [source precedence](references/house-principles.md#source-precedence)'s, 1 highest.
- `source`: where the rule comes from, then `; strength: ` and how strong that source is. The question's own level is read from it: level 1 where the strength begins `law` or `WCAG`, level 2 for any other project question, level 3 for a vault question whose strength begins `domain evidence`, and below level 2, at a level `list` does not read, for any other vault question, so a vault question that gives way to another vault question names its id. A vault question on behaviour (what a part does, how a visitor reaches it) that rests on domain evidence leaves `level 2` out of its `yields-to`: a project question that clashes with it goes to the founder, unless its own strength begins `domain evidence` too.
- `era`: `timeless`, or `dated <YYYY-MM-DD>`, the date to review it by.
- `owner`: `vault`, or the project's library key.

A record the vault owns also gives `stage`, `reasons`, `ledger` and `status`, and the rest where they apply. A project's question may give any of them, and is held to the same form where it does:

- `stage`: `function`, `role`, `treatment` or `value`, the step of the [dependency order](references/house-principles.md#dependency-order) it decides. `value` also holds a rule a script settles against a fixed bound or fact rather than a number the builder picks (`vault.reflow`'s widths, `vault.drafts-kept`, `vault.end-frame`): the builder reaches it as the script to run before any critic.
- `target`: what the building session builds to, in its own words rather than the check's. Required wherever the builder loads the record, every stage but `value` and any record answered by `builder`, and refused everywhere else.
- `inputs` and `output`, together or not at all: for a value several factors combine into, the inputs it reads and the formula or lookup table that gives it. One record owns each value property for each element role; `yields-to` stays for clashes between records.
- `script`: the script in `skills/design-round/scripts/` and its mode, or `owed` where none answers the rule yet. Required on a vault record answered by `script`, and refused on any other.
- `parts`, on a `treatment` record alone: the parts it applies to, comma-separated, so the builder's build stage is given it only for a candidate that has one of them. A record naming none is given for every candidate. The parts are a role `parts-map.py` gives a part a visitor can act on (`link`, `button`, `textbox`, `combobox`, `checkbox`, `radio`, `switch`, `slider`, `tab`, `menuitem`, `option`, `iframe`), or a part the building session declares (`hero`, `card`, `image`, `icon`, `logo`, `form`, `nav`, `dialog`, `toast`, `bottom-sheet`, `table`, `chart`, `video`, `badge`, `testimonial`, `primary-action`, `destructive-action`, `secondary-text`, `tag`, `list-header`, `service-bar`, `brand-bar`, `user-name`, `misspelling`, `pixel-image`, `cookie-banner`, `carousel`, `map`, `drag-item`, `pull-to-refresh`, `resizable-sheet`, `motion-control`, `offer-deadline`, `editable-value`, `zoomable`, `select-all`, `empty-state`, `category-tile`). The declaration is a JSON file, `{"declared": {<part>: <its CSS selector, or null>}}`, given to `list --parts` as a parts map is.
- `reasons`: the reference section holding the rule's reasons and trade-offs, `<file>#<heading anchor>`, the file's path from the plugin's `skills/` folder.
- `ledger`: the coverage ledger row keys it fills, comma-separated, or `none`.
- `status`: `live`, or `staged` for a record that reaches no agent until the load-change test for its tier passes (#258, the spec's §The load-change test).

A rule checked both by a script or the code and by the look is two questions, each with one answerer. The look half yields to the other half, which links them.

## How the records are answered

design-critique §1a says how each craft, function and proof question is run.

**The function walk's records** (`vault.affordance`, `vault.borrowing`, `vault.primary-action`, `vault.one-target`, `vault.high-stakes`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/parts-map.py <page> --out <dir>` maps each part of a page at desktop and phone width: from the DOM, whether a visitor can act on it, and the parts that could look as if they can. Its output answers `vault.one-target`, and `gate.js tally` compares the critics' part-by-part answers to `vault.affordance` with the map, and fails it on any link the map marks as going nowhere: to `#` alone, or to a part of the page that is not there. Each asks one or two questions of the house's [function walk](references/house-principles.md#the-function-walk). A minor case is reported and does not fail: the critic answers Yes, with evidence beginning `Minor:`.

**The access records** (the WCAG ones answered by `check-access.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-access.py <page> --out <dir> [--logotype <selector>]… [--parts <declaration>]` answers them at desktop and phone width. Pass one `--logotype` for each logotype selector in the project's rules file, under §4 Logo and marks. Where §4 has no `Logotype selectors:` line, add it from the site's markup before the run. Always pass the build's parts declaration as `--parts`, declaring `chart` and `carousel` null where the page has none: its chart is the one whose marks vault.chart-contrast reads, its carousel the one whose slides and page dots vault.controls-on-photo reads and whose focus vault.carousel-focus judges. A question it cannot measure goes to the gate critics.

**Targets by pointer** (`check-access.py --pointer`). `--pointer` answers `vault.coarse-target` alone, into `pointer.json`: it loads the page under a coarse and a fine pointer at each width. The coarse floor sits in `check-access-pointer.table` beside the script; where the project's rules file sets its own coarse value, copy that table with the value changed into the round folder and pass it as `--pointer-table`.

**The records that compare pages** (answered by `check-cross.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-cross.py <home page> --out <dir> [--flow <first step>]… [--form <selector>]` opens every page the home page's links reach on the same site and answers `vault.one-site`, `vault.nav-order` and `vault.multiple-ways`. Pass one `--flow` for each flow of more than one step, naming its first step, and `--form` where a step's first `<form>` is not the flow's own, such as a search form in the header; each flow answers `vault.flow-back` and `vault.prefilled-repeat`. The values each check reads sit in `check-cross.table` beside the script, keyed by record id. A flow that stops at its first step leaves its records not judged, and they go to the look critics.

**The DOM records** (answered by `check-dom.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-dom.py <page> --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>]` answers each record that has lines in `check-dom.table` at phone and desktop width, from the page's elements, attributes and head tags, and from what the page listens for. It also loads the page right to left, and asks the page's site for an address it does not have; a local file has no site, so `vault.missing-page` is not judged on one. Give it the building session's parts declaration: a part it does not name counts as absent, and one declared with no selector leaves its lines not judged, for the look critics.

**The markup records** (answered by `check-markup.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-markup.py <page> --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>]` runs the vendored axe-core (`skills/design-round/vendor/axe-core/`) at phone and desktop width and answers `vault.lang-parts`, `vault.heading-markup`, `vault.table-headers`, `vault.logo-link-name`, `vault.label-in-name`, `vault.landmarks` and `vault.progress-name`, each by the axe rule ids its line in `check-markup.table` names. Give it the building session's parts declaration, so `vault.logo-link-name` reads the logo; without one that record is not judged, and goes to the look critics. What axe could not decide is printed as review: it changes no answer and goes to no critic.

**The computed-style records** (answered by `check-style.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-style.py <page> --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>] [--brief energy=<calm, middle or loud>]` answers each record that has lines in `check-style.table`. A line that reads a part the declaration does not give is dropped, so a record with no other line answers no match. `vault.calm-colour-share` is not judged without `--brief energy=`.

**The text records** (answered by `check-text.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-text.py <page> --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>] [--words <file>]… [--own-case]` reads the page's words, characters, case, and number and date formats at phone and desktop width, right to left, and under the browser locales its table names, and answers each record that has lines in `check-text.table`. Its word lists (acronyms, proper nouns, units and the rest) sit in `check-text.words`; give `--words` a project's own list in that form, its names and acronyms above all, so they are not read as title case or typed capitals. Give it the building session's parts declaration: without an `offer-deadline` part `vault.offer-end-date` has no match, and declared with no selector it is not judged. Give `--own-case` where the project's rules file names a case other than sentence case: `vault.label-case` is then not judged, and goes to the gate critics.

**Enlarged and spaced text** (`check-layout.py --text`). `--text spacing` sweeps the page again with WCAG 1.4.12's four spacing overrides and answers `vault.text-spacing`; `--text zoom` (browser zoom) and `--text size` (a raised default text size) sweep it with text at 200%, for the records that judge text there. Each reports only the cut-off, overlapping or hidden text the page at 100% does not have at that width, and zoom and size list text that grows less than the rest. The values sit in `check-layout.table` beside the script, keyed by record id.

**The records on the files a page loads** (answered by `check-load.py --files`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-load.py <page> --files --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>]` loads the page once and reads every font, image, stylesheet and script file it fetches: it answers `vault.glyph-coverage`, `vault.glyph-distinct`, `vault.small-caps`, `vault.font-features`, `vault.figure-face`, `vault.type-axes`, `vault.icon-vector` and `vault.cookie-banner`. Give it the building session's parts declaration: without a `cookie-banner` in it, `vault.cookie-banner` is not judged. A line it leaves not judged goes to the critic the record's other half names: the type critic for a face, the code reader for `vault.cookie-banner`. The values sit in `check-load.table` beside the script, keyed by record id.

**The layout, reflow and fold records** (`check-layout.py --records`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-layout.py <page> --out <dir> --records [--parts <declaration>] [--brief kind=<app or site>] [--brief page=<reading, default or canvas>]` answers each record whose `script:` line names `--records`, across the same width sweep and on loads of its own: zoomed, with a larger default text size, with script off, under a phone's bottom inset, and with each popover and menu open. Its lines sit in `check-layout.table`; the phone-only checks judge under its `phone-width` line, the records' 600 px. Without the parts declaration the card and logo records are not judged, and without `--brief kind=` `vault.nav-by-width` is not, and each goes to the look critics; `--brief page=` sets the cap `vault.content-width` holds the main content to, the default page's where it is not given.

**The driven-state records** (answered by `check-state.py`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-state.py <page> --out <dir> [--parts <declaration>] [--handover <selector>]…` drives a fresh load of the page into each state only a visitor reaches: hover, focus, a press held down, a key, a submit, an open menu or dialog, a drag, a wheel, a pinch and the passing of time under a fake clock. It answers every record whose `script:` line names it, each by its line in `check-state.table`. It really fills and submits forms, so run it on a candidate, never a live site; it answers a link off the site with a blank page. Give it the building session's parts declaration: `vault.editable-cue` and `vault.pinch-anchor` read a declared part and are not judged without one, and the toast, video and select-all parts widen what their lines find. Pass `--handover` for each link the project's rules file keeps a handover notice for. A control `vault.on-input` finds acting on its own, where the page describes that behaviour before the control, is printed as review: it changes no answer.

**The motion records** (`check-flow.py --records`). `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-round/scripts/check-flow.py <page> --out <dir> --records [--parts <declaration>]` answers each record whose `script:` line names `--records`, each on loads of its own: a wheel turn, a Page Down and a touch drag for `vault.native-scroll`, a scroll down, back and down again for `vault.parallax`, a phone and a desktop watch of the carousel on the page's clock for `vault.carousel-timing`, a film of each screen at 1024 × 768 for `vault.flash-limit`, slow drags and a fling of each bottom sheet and card for `vault.drag-release`, a hover and a press of each link, button and menu item for `vault.motion-frequency`, and the page's style sheets, with its script told the screen updates slowly, for `vault.slow-update`. Its lines sit in `check-flow.table`. Without the parts declaration `vault.carousel-timing` and `vault.drag-release` are not judged, and go to the look critics; a swipe row is not read, since no part names one.
