Bali

Best Hostels in Canggu

Canggu is Bali's hub for surfers, remote workers and long-stay travellers: a stretch of rice-field backroads, beach clubs and cafés running from Berawa up to Echo Beach. It's busy, social and set up for people wanting to stay and live in the area for a while.

Surf

Forgiving beach breaks at Batu Bolong (Old Man's) and Echo Beach make it one of the easiest places in Bali to learn or progress, with boards and lessons within walking distance of most hostels. Best for beginners and intermediates.

Vibe

Lively and social, leaning digital-nomad and party crowd: co-working spaces, sunset beach bars and a constant turnover of new faces.

What to look for

How close it is to Batu Bolong or Berawa, whether there's a pool and reliable wifi if you're working, and whether it's a party hostel or a quiet one. Canggu has plenty of both.
