Records for the type critic, web-surface, has-cta, coarse to fine: 14 record(s).

1. vault.heading-step (role): Does each heading stand apart from its body text by a step in size, weight, space or tone, any one of them carrying the step on its own?
   Applies to: every heading against the body text below it
2. vault.emphasis-cues (role): Is text ranked first by weight and a colour step and by size only in small steps (a key figure in a tile or chart free to take a display size), does each emphasised run take one cue, de-emphasised text never both a lighter colour and a size under the body floor, and does each view hold no more than three levels of text contrast?
   Applies to: any text ranked above or below the text around it: a key figure, an emphasised phrase, metadata, secondary lines, and the text styles of each view
3. vault.face-roles (role): Does each typeface hold one job kept across the site (body, headings or display, interface, code), two families at most besides a monospace face that sets only code, sample text and the compared numbers `vault.figure-face` sends it, with an app's working surfaces in its interface face and a brand or display face kept to marketing and editorial surfaces?
   Applies to: every typeface the page loads
4. vault.paragraph-break (treatment): Are paragraphs set apart by space between them or by a first-line indent, never both?
   Applies to: every run of paragraphs
5. vault.no-justify (treatment): Is running text set flush to its reading side and ragged at the other, never justified?
   Applies to: every block of running text
6. vault.dashes (treatment): Does each dash do its one job: a hyphen inside a compound word, an en dash for a range or a pairing (1880–1912, London–Paris), an em dash for a break in a sentence, never a hyphen or a double hyphen for either dash?
   Applies to: every hyphen and dash in prose, headings and labels
7. vault.prose-emphasis (treatment): Within running text, is emphasis bold or italic and never both, never an underline, kept to a few key words or phrases rather than long runs, with bold rather than italic in a sans serif and italic kept for titles and quotations there?
   Applies to: emphasis inside running text
8. vault.dna-caps-label (treatment): Was each label the candidate sets in capitals asked for by the brief or the rules file?
   Applies to: every label of under one line set in capitals, on a project that takes the House DNA
9. vault.dark-text-weight (treatment): Does light text on a dark ground keep its weight at small sizes, large text being free to go one step lighter, while taking the extra line height and tracking impeccable `typeset` gives it?
   Applies to: light text on a dark ground
10. vault.glyph-distinct (treatment): Does the text face draw I, l and 1 as three distinct shapes, and where text holds codes, references or data a visitor copies or types, is 0 told from O by a slashed or dotted zero or a clearly different shape?
   Applies to: the face chosen for text, and text holding codes, references or data a visitor reads character by character
11. vault.figure-face (treatment): Does the face that sets compared or updating numbers have tabular figures, by default or by `tnum`, or, where it has none, are those numbers moved to a face that has them or set in a fixed-width box?
   Applies to: a face that sets numbers compared down a column or list, or updating in place
12. vault.measure (value): Does every full line of running text hold 50–75 characters, spaces included, counted as the page sets them rather than worked out from a CSS width?
   Applies to: every full line of running text, at every width the page's breakpoints make
13. vault.body-size (value): Is body text 16 px or larger, smaller only in a dense role that justifies it, and is text at phone widths 16 px or larger?
   Applies to: body text, and all text at phone widths
14. vault.leading (value): Does body text take a line height of 1.3 or more that opens up as its line lengthens, with only headings and display type set tighter?
   Applies to: body text
