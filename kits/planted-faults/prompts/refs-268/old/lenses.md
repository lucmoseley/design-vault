# The added lenses (design-critique §2, the judgement half)

**Gestalt, by name.** Walk all seven, and for each one say what you found or that you found nothing: proximity · similarity · closure · continuity · common region · figure/ground · common fate (parts that move or change together, at the same moment, read as one group).

**Conversion and trust.** The project's copy bans, from the brief, win over this lens: a point below that they ban is no finding.
- The 5-second test: above the fold, can a reader say what this is, who it is for and why they should care?
- CTA count and the friction budget.
- Risk-reversal microcopy.
- Objection handling.
- Credibility, and who is behind the product. On a small business's or a solo advisor's site, judge it by first-visit trust (first-visit-trust.md).

Report absent social proof as a finding. Never fix it by inventing proof.

**SEO.** The heading outline, internal linking, and above all whether the `h1`'s promise matches what the page actually contains.
