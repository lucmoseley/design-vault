Gate list for web-surface, has-cta: 19 question(s).

Look questions, for the critics, pass 1 of 4, function and role: function: data, forms, imagery, interaction, layout, navigation, system:

12. vault.table-markup-look: Is content set in rows and columns built as a `table` of `tr`, `th` and `td`, or with the ARIA table roles, never as a grid of plain `div`s?
   Applies to: content set in rows and columns a visitor reads across and down
19. vault.auth-paste-look: Does no step ask for chosen characters of a password, and where a step blocks paste or a password manager, does it offer another way to sign in that needs no remembering or transcription?
   Applies to: every sign-in, sign-up or verification step
20. vault.redundant-entry: Within one process, is every answer the visitor already gave that is not filled in for them offered to pick (a "same as" checkbox, a carried-forward answer, an earlier upload to reuse), save where re-entry is essential, needed for security, or the earlier answer no longer valid?
   Applies to: every process that asks a visitor for the same information twice: a second address, a repeated upload, a later step needing an earlier answer
21. vault.keep-answers: Where a form is long, can the visitor save and resume it, and does a problem page say what happened to their answers?
   Applies to: every form, above all a long or multi-step one
22. vault.form-help: Does a long or complex form offer a way to get help that keeps the visitor on the form, with more help offered where they hit the same error again?
   Applies to: every long or complex form
23. vault.sign-in: Can every sign-in step be passed without remembering, transcribing or solving anything, save recognising objects or the visitor's own pictures: paste and password-manager autofill allowed (`autocomplete="username"`, `current-password`, `one-time-code`), the password showable, and a passkey, an email link or a third-party sign-in offered beside any puzzle or code to retype?
   Applies to: every sign-in, sign-up and verification step
24. vault.time-limit-look: Where a time limit gives no warning with one action to extend it, can the visitor turn it off or set it to at least ten times its length, or is it a real-time event, an essential limit or one over 20 hours, and does an automatic redirect give "Go now" and "Stay" controls?
   Applies to: every time limit the page sets: a session timeout, an automatic redirect, a timed step
25. vault.one-thing-per-page: Is a long form split across pages that each hold one thing (one piece of information, one decision or one question), with pages merged only where user research shows the visitors answer them together, and is no page cut down by spreading the same fields over more steps rather than dropping the fields the task does not need?
   Applies to: every form longer than one screen of questions, checkout included
29. vault.icon-use: Does each icon mark an action or a piece of information a visitor looks for (a phone number, a download, a share) rather than decorate, take the conventional symbol for that one meaning and no other, and carry a visible text label, save the few icons nearly everyone knows (home, print, the magnifier for search) and the icon-only controls `vault.carry-act` allows, each with an accessible name?
   Applies to: every icon on the page other than a logo
31. vault.image-earns: Does each image carry information the visitor uses (the product, the place, the real person, the evidence) or a feeling the brief names, none being generic stock or AI filler set to fill space?
   Applies to: every photo and illustration on the page that is not an icon or the logo
36. vault.affordance: Does every part a visitor can act on look as if they can, and every part they cannot look as if they cannot, save a hidden link that goes where a part that does look actable also goes, which is reported and does not fail?
   Applies to: each part on the parts map's key, at desktop and phone width, answered part by part
37. vault.whole-target-look: Does each accordion heading and chip that does one thing take a press anywhere it is drawn, its words, icon and count included, and not only where its words sit?
   Applies to: every composite part that does one thing and is no row, list item or article: an accordion heading, a chip
38. vault.drag-alternative: Can everything a drag or a multipoint gesture such as a pinch does also be done by single taps or clicks with no drag or multipoint gesture (a tap on the slider track, move-up and move-down buttons, a "Choose file" button beside the drop zone, previous and next buttons, map pan and zoom buttons, a refresh button, a tap on the sheet's grabber), keyboard support alone not counting?
   Applies to: every part the page lets a visitor drag: a slider, a reorderable list, a drop zone, a swipe carousel or row, a map, a resizable sheet, a custom pull-to-refresh; and every multipoint gesture the page reads, such as a pinch zoom
39. vault.motion-input: Can each thing device or body motion does also be done with an on-screen control, and can the visitor turn the response to motion off?
   Applies to: every feature worked by shaking, tilting or moving the device or the body
45. vault.stack-order: When the page falls to one column, do its parts come in the page's priority order (the brief's ranking of the visitor's tasks, the main content before its supporting pane), with no group's members pushed apart by the stacking?
   Applies to: the page at each width where it falls to one column, at phone width and at 200% text
48. vault.exit-warning-look: Does each link that leaves the site tell its destination by its link text and an external-link indicator?
   Applies to: every link or button that takes the visitor to another site
49. vault.carousel-use: Is a hero one static image or message and a set of items a grid or list, a carousel used only for a set the visitor browses one at a time that the page cannot lay out (a listing's photos)?
   Applies to: every hero, and every set of like items shown on the page
51. vault.once-only-back: After an action in a flow that must happen once, does the browser's Back button still work and show a sensible message, rather than repeat the action?
   Applies to: every flow step that follows an action that must happen once: a payment taken, a booking or form sent
62. vault.borrowing: Does each part show its function only by that function's own look, never one another kind of part keeps for itself (a static label shaped and filled like the button, a heading in the link colour, a fact in the filter chip's shape), with each distinct look meaning one function across the page?
   Applies to: every part, against every other part on the page
