## Brief delta, round 2

**From the founder (his words are on #8; these are the lines that bind):**
- **The personal-advisor test comes first, before craft.** Mark is a "personal expert advisor"; "the firm is the brand" fails (brand sheet §5). A tile passes when:
  - Mark is seen: his photograph is given real weight;
  - Mark is heard: first person, and the heading is his voice, not a label;
  - his name leads, with the logo beside it;
  - there is one direct, personal way to reach him.

  It fails on a firm's "we", a corporate tagline, stock imagery, or a look any consultancy could own.
- **Refined, never childish.** No handwriting, novelty or "fun" faces; no phrasing that reads as a joke out of context. The "Whichever PPP Law…" quote is used only with its set-up, never alone in a display face.
- **Reflects what Mark does:** law, PPP and advice to governments. He is not an engineer or architect, so nothing reads as "in-your-face techy".
- **The house colours:** blue `#0070C0` and grey `#404040`. The founder likes them, and every tile is built in or around them.
- **Alive, not boring.** v5 House colours had fine parts put together dully.
- **Legal pad and blueprint are saveable with careful intention.** Three things to get right on each:
  - the exact colours and shades;
  - how busy or quiet the ground is;
  - where, if anywhere, and how its borders appear.

  Start from the restraint of the animation storyboard's round one (`rounds/aqueduct-storyboard-drafts`, `r1/v1-legal-pad`, `r1/v2-blueprint`):
  - one soft ground at low contrast;
  - the house blue and grey as the only ink;
  - serious type;
  - at most one borrowed detail of the real object.
- **Photos:** Mark's real photographs from the current site stand in until the image round (Q2 (A)). Each carries a small caption saying so.

**From the research (`docs/research/site-look-precedent.md`):**
- One very large serif line first: scale contrast, not more elements (synthesis a.1, b).
- A ground, an ink and at most one accent (a.2).
- Real people in real light (a.3); imagery graded into the house colours (F4).
- One message and one action on the first screen (a.4).
- A warm paper ground rather than `#FFFFFF`, except where a tile's lever is the ground itself (b).
- Depth after the first look: the article specimen and the PDF list are the product (a.6).
- What fails: childish, cheesy, slick, salesy (a.7).
- Measured: `#0070C0` on white is 5.15:1 and on `#F8F7EE` 4.79:1. Blue on `#404040` is 2.01:1, so never set blue on the grey.

## Brief

**Aesthetic** — seven directions:

- **v1 Counsel's paper** — the legal pad, refined. Precedents: Freshfields F10, Harvey F5, Kümmerlein F1.
  - Warm ivory paper and one hairline frame like a document's edge.
  - Pale legal yellow used only as a colour memory, behind one quotation or the contact panel.
  - Grey ink, blue on the wordmark and links.
  - No rules, pad, binding, clip, handwriting or stack.
- **v2 Night drawing** — the blueprint, refined, on the darker backdrop the founder suggested. Precedents: storyboard `r1/v2-blueprint`, McKinsey's deep blue F13, Drawing Matter's restraint F16.
  - A deep navy derived from the house blue; at most a ghost of a grid.
  - The aqueduct as one fine pale line drawing, the one borrowed detail.
  - White serif type and the house blue as accent.
  - No dimensions, centre lines, mono or tracked techy capitals.
  - Article pages are light.
- **v3 The annotated brief** — the page set like a legal journal. Precedents: Harvey F5, Drawing Matter F16, IMF F&D F17.
  - A wide serif text column.
  - Small blue sans margin notes giving the place and year of his work.
  - Real items, such as a photograph or a page of his slides, shown framed as objects.
- **v4 House colours, dark ground** — v5 of round 1 rebuilt, its lever the ground: deep charcoal or navy, white serif type, logo blue as accent.
- **v5 House colours, one blue field** — its lever a colour field: the first screen one field of `#0070C0` (or a deepened house blue) with white serif type, white below. Precedents: Kea F7, McKinsey F13.
- **v6 House colours, the portrait** — its lever the person: warm off-white, with Mark's studio portrait at full height carrying the right of the page. Precedents: Dualite (round 1), Haavind F2.
- **v7 Mark at work** — new, built from scratch rather than on v5. Precedents: Kümmerlein F1, Cornerstone's tinting F4, JRF F11.

# MMM Infra — brand sheet

The client's own rules for design rounds. In the design vault this project is `mmm-infra`; a verdict cites a section here as `fit.mmm-infra.sheet_section`. "Open" marks what Mark has not ruled yet, and names the ticket that settles it.

## §1 Who it is for

- **Client:** Mark M Moseley, trading as MMM Infra (Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services). He is an infrastructure advisory consultant with a legal background.
- **Audience:** staff at multilateral development banks and other development agencies who may be looking for an infrastructure advisory consultant (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **The one action:** get in touch with Mark. This is assumed, not yet confirmed by Mark (open: #10, Dad's questionnaire).

## §2 Colour

These values were measured on the current site and logo. Mark has not ruled on whether they carry over (open: #8, the visual direction, put to Mark on #10).

- Logo blue `#0070C0`: the wordmark.
- Logo grey `#404040`: the aqueduct arches.
- Site text `#3F3F3F`, panel grey `#595959`, ground `#FFFFFF`.

For the opening animation, Mark suggested white, a muted colour, blueprint paper or a lined yellow legal pad as the background, and leaned towards the legal pad "given my legal background" (open: #9, the animation storyboard).

## §3 Type

- Logo: Georgia Pro, according to the logo file's name ("MMM Infra Full Logo (Georgia Pro)").
- Current site: Cormorant Garamond Semibold for headings; Proxima Nova and Roboto Thin for body text.

Open: Mark has not ruled on type (#8, put to Mark on #10).

## §4 Logo and marks

- The mark: three arches of an aqueduct over "MMM INFRA", with "Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services" set to the right.
- File: `docs/brand-assets/mmm-infra-full-logo.jpg`, a 1896 × 452 JPEG taken from the Wix site. It is the best copy held. The original artwork is to be asked for on Dad's questionnaire (#10).

## §5 Voice

- A personal, experienced advisor: the individual is the brand. The copy is approachable and emphasises experience and judgement (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- It is not the established-consultancy voice, which is corporate, polished and reserved and makes the firm the brand. The current tagline, "A Global Consulting Firm for Infrastructure Projects and Enabling Environments", speaks in that firm voice.
- Words to avoid: open (#10).

## §6 Standing rules

- **A brochure site:** no shop, no payments and no bespoke software (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **Angélique must be able to update it easily**, for example when Mark adds new articles (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- **Refresh the images**, possibly with images from Mark's LinkedIn posts (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **Video is welcome; busyness and advocacy are not.** Mark likes the videos on MacGeorge's and Threlfall's sites. He finds MacGeorge's site "much too busy", and Threlfall's reads as "an advocacy platform as opposed to a marketing vehicle" (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- **The opening animation follows the spirit of Mark's ideas, not the letter** (founder ruling, 4 Oct 2026, #9). His ideas are suggestions: the aqueduct built in a realistic sequence, in under a minute; "MMM Infra" then below it and "Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services" to its right; a dissolve to the homepage; no construction workers; a crane or other equipment moving in and out (Mark's email). Other ideas are open, such as starting from the logo and turning it into something else. The pipeline is decided on #5.
- **LoveFrom means only "smooth animation built around the aqueduct".** Read nothing else into it, and keep it out of the design vault (founder ruling on #1).

## §7 Image and video tools

Founder ruling, 4 Oct 2026 (#9), all through Higgsfield via the design vault's `higgsfield.js`:

- **Pictures:** GPT Image 2.5, the design vault's default.
- **Films that move through a scene** (a camera glide, a scene changing into another): Seedance. This overrides the vault's "drop" on Seedance for this project.
- **Short looping background clips:** Kling 3.0, the vault's pick; Seedance costs about 1.8 times as much.
- An AI clip can start exactly on a given picture, the logo included, but only lands close to an end picture. A build that must end exactly on the logo stays hand-built SVG (#5).
