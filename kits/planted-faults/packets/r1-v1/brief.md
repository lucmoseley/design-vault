## Direction cards

### v1 Legal pad

- **Evidence** — `site-kellywallace`: drawings sit on the speckled paper unframed and trail off at their edges, so they belong to the page rather than being placed on it.
- **Carry over** — a drawing that belongs to the paper: the aqueduct sketched straight onto the pad in ballpoint, unframed, its ends trailing off.
- **Invent** — Mark's suggestion, made into the whole ground: a canary legal pad, pale blue rules, the double red margin line, the gummed binding as the nav strip; ballpoint blue-black ink for type; Mark's hand for one note per view, the call to action written in the margin. The lawyer's working paper, where advice is drafted.
- **Avoid repeating** — the watercolour, the script statement laid over a photograph, the warm craft palette, the typewriter labels. Mark's own MacGeorge reference adds: no darkened portrait, translucent panels, pill buttons or clock.
- **Build scope** — palette, type pair, texture, nav bar, heading, button, article specimen, motion sketch.
- **Model** — none (code-drawn tile).

### v2 General arrangement

- **Evidence** — `richard-threlfall`: one full-screen picture before any text; text that arrives late and quietly; a thin bar of bold spaced capitals.
- **Carry over** — the picture leads and the words wait: the drawing fills the sheet first, and the heading arrives after it; navigation in spaced capitals.
- **Invent** — Mark's "blueprint paper" as a working engineering sheet: diazo-blue ground, white linework, a section cut with solid poche, dimension lines, and a title block (drawing number, scale, sheet) that carries the nav and the contact. Raised by the dealt section-drawing world (daylight section, competitive): one sodium-amber band marks the live part, as the sun marks its room.
- **Avoid repeating** — the Earth from orbit, black starfield, the countdown, the blurred slogan.
- **Build scope** — as v1.
- **Model** — none.

### v3 Inscription

- **Evidence** — none cited: the assigned grounded direction (concept-seed 20337a63, candidate 5 of my list: "the Roman aqueduct in carved stone, inscriptional capitals").
- **Carry over** — none (no reference).
- **Invent** — the aqueduct's own stone: cool grey dressed granite (Segovia, not Pont du Gard's cream), Roman square capitals for display with letters painted minium red as Roman carvers did, words parted by interpuncts, a tabula ansata panel (the dovetail-handled inscription tablet) as the button and the pull quote. Raised by the dealt challengers: *seven-segment* (declined) — absence is drawn: unbuilt voussoirs show as ghost joints; *toyism plate* (declined) — a fixed three-colour palette with hard edges and no blending; *kiln shelf* (declined) — one accent only, and it flows to the action.
- **Avoid repeating** — n/a (no reference). The literal reading, kept to this one direction.
- **Build scope** — as v1.
- **Model** — none.

### v4 Water

- **Evidence** — `prompting-company`: real infrastructure rising from the bottom edge into a calm, muted sky that holds the one headline and the one button.
- **Carry over** — the structure low and the words high: the aqueduct rises from the bottom edge, and the empty sky above it is the quiet field for Mark's name, his line and one button.
- **Invent** — the hue is the aqueduct's purpose, water: a muted river green (Mark's "muted colour"), the photo duotoned from deep pool to pale shallows; the second surface is the colour of still water, not charcoal; a fine ripple rule as the only ornament.
- **Avoid repeating** — the orange tower in fog, the centred startup layout (the words sit left in the sky), the badge and the trial buttons. Mark's own Infralegal reference adds: no single saturated wash such as its burnt orange.
- **Build scope** — as v1.
- **Model** — none (public-domain photo, duotoned in CSS/SVG, as a stand-in).

### v5 House colours

- **Evidence** — `dualite`: a white page, an old-style serif headline in two lines, one blue button, and one figure at full height bleeding off the right edge, given clearly less width than the words.
- **Carry over** — white, a serif headline in Mark's own words, one blue button, and Mark's photograph at full height off the right edge at unequal weight. Mark's own current site adds the logo's blue and grey and one square "Get in touch".

# The project's rules file: brand sheet

# MMM Infra — brand sheet

The client's own rules for design rounds. In the design vault this project is `mmm-infra`; a verdict cites a section here as `fit.mmm-infra.sheet_section`. "Open" marks what Mark has not ruled yet, and names the ticket that settles it.

## §1 Who it is for

- **Client:** Mark M Moseley, trading as MMM Infra (Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services). He is an infrastructure advisory consultant with a legal background.
- **Audience:** staff at multilateral development banks and other development agencies who may be looking for an infrastructure advisory consultant (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **The one action:** get in touch with Mark. This is assumed, not yet confirmed by Mark (open: #10, Dad's questionnaire).

## §2 Colour

These values were measured on the current site and logo. Mark has not ruled on whether they carry over (open: #8, the visual direction, put to Mark on #10).

- Logo blue `#0070C0`: the wordmark.
- Logo grey `#404040`: the aqueduct arches.
- Site text `#3F3F3F`, panel grey `#595959`, ground `#FFFFFF`.

For the opening animation, Mark suggested white, a muted colour, blueprint paper or a lined yellow legal pad as the background, and leaned towards the legal pad "given my legal background" (open: #9, the animation storyboard).

## §3 Type

- Logo: Georgia Pro, according to the logo file's name ("MMM Infra Full Logo (Georgia Pro)").
- Current site: Cormorant Garamond Semibold for headings; Proxima Nova and Roboto Thin for body text.

Open: Mark has not ruled on type (#8, put to Mark on #10).

## §4 Logo and marks

- The mark: three arches of an aqueduct over "MMM INFRA", with "Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services" set to the right.
- File: `docs/brand-assets/mmm-infra-full-logo.jpg`, a 1896 × 452 JPEG taken from the Wix site. It is the best copy held. The original artwork is to be asked for on Dad's questionnaire (#10).

## §5 Voice

- A personal, experienced advisor: the individual is the brand. The copy is approachable and emphasises experience and judgement (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- It is not the established-consultancy voice, which is corporate, polished and reserved and makes the firm the brand. The current tagline, "A Global Consulting Firm for Infrastructure Projects and Enabling Environments", speaks in that firm voice.
- Words to avoid: open (#10).

## §6 Standing rules

- **A brochure site:** no shop, no payments and no bespoke software (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **Angélique must be able to update it easily**, for example when Mark adds new articles (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- **Refresh the images**, possibly with images from Mark's LinkedIn posts (Mark, 25 July 2026).
- **Video is welcome; busyness and advocacy are not.** Mark likes the videos on MacGeorge's and Threlfall's sites. He finds MacGeorge's site "much too busy", and Threlfall's reads as "an advocacy platform as opposed to a marketing vehicle" (Mark, 26 July 2026).
- **The opening animation follows the spirit of Mark's ideas, not the letter** (founder ruling, 4 Oct 2026, #9). His ideas are suggestions: the aqueduct built in a realistic sequence, in under a minute; "MMM Infra" then below it and "Moseley Infrastructure Advisory Services" to its right; a dissolve to the homepage; no construction workers; a crane or other equipment moving in and out (Mark's email). Other ideas are open, such as starting from the logo and turning it into something else. The pipeline is decided on #5.
- **LoveFrom means only "smooth animation built around the aqueduct".** Read nothing else into it, and keep it out of the design vault (founder ruling on #1).

## §7 Image and video tools

Founder ruling, 4 Oct 2026 (#9), all through Higgsfield via the design vault's `higgsfield.js`:

- **Pictures:** GPT Image 2.5, the design vault's default.
- **Films that move through a scene** (a camera glide, a scene changing into another): Seedance. This overrides the vault's "drop" on Seedance for this project.
- **Short looping background clips:** Kling 3.0, the vault's pick; Seedance costs about 1.8 times as much.
- An AI clip can start exactly on a given picture, the logo included, but only lands close to an end picture. A build that must end exactly on the logo stays hand-built SVG (#5).
