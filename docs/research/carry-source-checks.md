## 5. What the checks changed

- **Font smoothing.** `vault.font-smoothing` rests only on what every source agrees on, greyscale smoothing on light text on dark, and leaves site-wide use to the project.
- **Tags are not capsules everywhere.** Atlassian Radius gives tags 4 px and Material's filter chip a small rounded rectangle, against Apple's capsule in the live `vault.radius-density`; its reasons now name both.

## 6. Bound

- The design-coverage-gaps sources were read through a summarising fetch, so their quotes are as it returned them; the Apple HIG text was read raw.
- **The house's own figures, not a source's:** the ~12:1 aim in `vault.body-ink` and `vault.text-steps` rests on Legge read second-hand (research B1.2) and the Typography Handbook's #333 example; `vault.text-on-dark`'s 7:1 for small secondary text applies Apple's general "strive for 7:1"; `vault.dark-ground`'s OKLCH 0.18–0.22 converts the Typography Handbook's #121212–#1a1a1a; `vault.carousel-timing`'s max(5, ⌈words ÷ 3⌉) s joins NN/g's reading rate to Baymard's 5 s floor; `vault.validation-timing` splits GOV.UK (on submit, one-question pages) from Baymard and NN/g (on leaving the field, longer forms) by the form each tested.
- **Unmeasured:** `vault.fluid-type`'s owed script models 200% zoom as half the viewport with the root size doubled; it should be confirmed against a real zoom.
