The source checks and browser measurements behind tier 0 jobs ranks 5–8 of the coverage ledger: typography-defaults, forms-defaults, text-defaults and imagery-defaults. Read 19 Oct 2026. Measurements ran in headless Chromium 149.0.7827.0 under Playwright for Python on this machine (WSL2); Firefox and Safari are not installed here. The measurement scripts are on #276.

#### Research lines for TYS-53

- `docs/research/notes-coverage.md:48` says "WCAG 1.4.12 at 0.12 em user spacing". That matches 1.4.12, but it leaves out that the SC tests all four overrides together, with nothing else changed. `vault.text-spacing` carries the 0.12 em claim inside the full four-value test. The rest of the line (Butterick on lowercase letterspacing, impeccable's tracking detectors) is about letter-spacing the author sets. `vault.type-tracking` owns that, so the new record does not carry it.
- `docs/research/text-presentation.md:160` says "WCAG 1.4.12: layout must survive user letter spacing of 0.12 em (P)". It is narrower than the SC in two ways: the test sets all four values at once, and the SC covers markup text only. `vault.text-spacing` carries the claim in full. The line's capitals tracking (Butterick 5–12%, Bringhurst 5–10%) belongs to `vault.type-tracking` (+0.05 to +0.10 em), so it is not carried here.
- The ledger note "0.12 em survival stays research" is answered once `vault.text-spacing` goes live. `vault.reflow` (live, layout.md) still lists TYS-53 in its `ledger` and says it "does not apply 1.4.12's spacing overrides". When the new record goes live, TYS-53 should move off `vault.reflow`'s ledger.
