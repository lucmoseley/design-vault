## 2. Measurement

Headless Chromium 149.0.7827.0 under Playwright for Python, on this machine (WSL2). Firefox and Safari were not run. The script is on #277.

### 2.1 A drag past the control's edge

A 100 × 20 px "slider" holding the text "Drag this card", a 300 × 60 px box with a red `:hover` below it, and a paragraph below that. The mouse pressed on the slider's text, moved 5 steps over the box, read the box's background, then moved 10 steps over the paragraph and released. Moves counted on the slider's own `pointermove` listener.

| Setup | Slider moves past its edge | Box mid-drag | Text selected |
|---|---|---|---|
| no capture | 1 | red (hovered) | "Drag th" |
| `setPointerCapture` | 16 | white | "is card" |
| capture and `user-select: none` on the body during the drag | 16 | white | none |
| capture and `preventDefault()` on `pointerdown` | 16 | white | none |
