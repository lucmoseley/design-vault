## 2. Where design-feel.md does not hold

- **Colourfulness does not fall steeply.** `design-feel.md:84` and the Part C table say appeal rises to moderate-to-high colourfulness "before steeply declining". The full text gives d 0.3 below the peak and 0.8 above, and says people "do not seem to respond as dramatically" to colourfulness as to complexity. The steep cost is complexity's (d 2.0 above its peak, 0.6 below).
- **"Symmetric" as a classical item is unconfirmed.** `design-feel.md:147` puts it on Lavie and Tractinsky's classical scale through Moshagen and Thielsch's quotation; no page opened here shows the item, and the 2004 paper returned 403. `vault.tone-symmetry` rests on NN/g and Wu instead.
- **The premium readings are not carried.** `design-feel.md:146` and `:151` also rest on a 2023 Chinese packaging study (white space and purchase intention) and a 2022 packaging study (symmetry and premiumness). Neither was opened here; both measure packs, not pages, so no record claims white space or symmetry reads premium.
- **Qiu et al.'s type factors are unconfirmed.** The full text would not download, and the abstract gives only the method, so `vault.tone-face` does not cite the elegance and practicality factors.
