# design-vault

The founder's design method and inspiration library, shared by every project he designs.

## Language

**Candidate**:
An asset a design round makes, rendered for the founder to judge against a brief.
_Avoid_: draft, option, variant (a variant is one kind of candidate)

**Review page**:
A page that shows the founder many items, one card each with a feedback box, whose answers a session reads back.
_Avoid_: calibration page, scores page, feedback page

**Client asset**:
A photo, logo, live page or text the client already has, as against a candidate a round makes.
_Avoid_: supplied asset, existing asset

**Judging in place**:
Judging a client asset where it is used now, for the question the client asked.
_Avoid_: re-evaluation, audit

**Choosing and preparing**:
Picking client assets for a new design, then cropping and editing them for their slots.
_Avoid_: selection, curation

**Tone**:
The feel a design aims at — calm or loud, light or heavy, orderly or rich — as positions on scales, with the choices that move it there.
_Avoid_: energy, vibe, register (impeccable's plain/safer/bolder re-roll), mood (kept for images)

**Design decision**:
One choice a designer makes on one property of one element in one context, such as the letter-spacing of a large heading.
_Avoid_: topic, area, subject (a subject only groups decisions)

**Coverage ledger**:
The list of every design decision the house's research must cover, one row each.
_Avoid_: coverage frame, gap list, topic list

**Covered**:
Said of a design decision whose house rule is sourced, names what changes its answer and what it trades against, is marked timeless or era-bound, and reaches the builder or critic of a web round.
_Avoid_: mentioned, handled (a mention, a project's own value or a ban alone is not coverage)

**Source family**:
A kind of source that names design decisions in its own way, such as craft books, design systems or browser documentation.
_Avoid_: source type, source set

**Method miss**:
A design decision the founder raises that the coverage ledger did not hold.
_Avoid_: new topic, gap (a gap is a row the ledger holds but the house does not cover)

**Whole-page critic**:
A critic that judges a candidate as a whole — what each part is for, what stands out, how busy it is, whether it hangs together — and names the leader, leaving every yes/no rule to the gate and the type rules to the type critic.
_Avoid_: critique critic, main critic, fresh critic (every critic is fresh)

**Type critic**:
A critic that judges only a candidate's lettering — its typefaces, sizes, weights, spacing and emphasis, and whether like text is set alike — beside the whole-page critic, and names no leader.
_Avoid_: font critic, typography reviewer

**Gate critic**:
One of three blind critics that answer only the gate's look questions, Yes, No or Unsure, with no critique, score or leader.
_Avoid_: look critic, checker

**Rule record**:
One house rule written once: when it applies, its stage, who answers it, what to build, and the question that checks it.
_Avoid_: rule entry, principle (a principle in research may yield several rule records)
